# Nut Butter Cookies

## Ingredients
* 1 large egg
* 1 cup of nut butter of choice
* 1 cup of brown sugar 

## Instructions 
* preheat oven to 180C 
* combine all ingredients in mixing bowl
* roll into 2cm balls 
* use a fork to lighltly press the ball
* Bake for 10-12 min (or until they look dry on top)
